Privacy policy

This personal Home Assistant integration accesses Google Nest device data only after the owner authorizes it with Google.

Data is used solely to display and control the owner's selected Nest devices in the owner's private Home Assistant installation. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

The Home Assistant installation is privately operated by the owner. Access may be revoked at any time through the Google Account permissions page or by removing the integration.
